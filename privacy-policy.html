Introduction

Just Split Bills ("Just Split," "we," "our," or "us") is a mobile application designed to help you split expenses with friends and groups. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Just Split Bills application (the "App"), available on the Apple App Store.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to provide, maintain, and improve the App:

  • Account creation and authentication — verifying your identity via Apple Sign-In and phone number verification
  • Core app functionality — creating groups, recording expenses, calculating balances, and tracking settlements
  • Group member matching — using normalised phone numbers to connect you with friends within expense groups
  • Push notifications — sending you activity updates and reminders related to your groups
  • Data synchronisation — keeping your data consistent across your devices
  • Preference persistence — remembering your theme and currency preferences

We do not use your information for advertising, behavioural profiling, data brokering, or sale to third parties under any circumstances.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in two locations:

  • On-device (local) — Expenses, groups, settlements, and receipt images are stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework within the App's sandboxed container. Authentication credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain, which is hardware-encrypted by the operating system.
  • Cloud (remote) — Account information, groups, expenses, and settlements are synced to Google Cloud Firestore, where data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).

We implement reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your data. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

  • Account data (name, email, phone number) — retained until you delete your account.
  • Expense and group data — retained until you delete individual items or your account.
  • Soft-deleted expenses — moved to "Recently Deleted" and permanently purged after 30 days.
  • Push notification tokens — removed from our servers when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Keychain data — cleared automatically by the App upon reinstallation.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access — View all your personal data within the App at any time via the Settings and group screens.
  • Correction — Update your name and preferences in the App's Settings.
  • Deletion — Delete your account from within the App (Settings → Delete Account). This permanently removes your account, expense history, and group memberships from our servers. Shared expense splits involving other members may be retained in anonymised form to maintain the integrity of other members' records.
  • Data export — Request a complete export of your data by emailing privacy@justsplitapp.com.
  • Withdraw permissions — Revoke camera, contacts, or notification permissions at any time via iOS Settings → Just Split Bills. The App will continue to function with reduced features.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@justsplitapp.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at privacy@justsplitapp.com, and we will promptly delete such information.

By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries outside your country of residence, which may have different data protection laws. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place through our service providers' compliance with applicable data protection frameworks.
